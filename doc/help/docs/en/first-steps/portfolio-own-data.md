# Your portfolio in KANAP in ten minutes

[Explore with the example](portfolio-example.md) · **Start with my own data**

In ten minutes, a first request is scored and converted into a project, the project has its first tasks and its budget, and your contributor profile is ready for planning. Starting with your own data erases the example: what you see at the end comes from you.

These are the five steps of the **Your first five steps** tile on the home page, for the path **Projects and requests** with **Start with my own data**. Each step in the tile opens its screen with a short hint. Click **Done** in the hint, or the mark next to the step, once you have done it. See [Trial: your path and first steps](../my-dashboard.md#trial-first-steps).

## 1. Your first request

**Portfolio > Requests**

**What to do.** Click **New request**, type its name in the title and press Enter. Answer each criterion in the **Scoring** tab. Set its status to **Approved**, then click **Convert to project** and **Create project**.

**What you see.** The request exists as soon as it has a name, with the status **Pending review** and you as the requestor. The score is recomputed at each answer. The criteria and their weights are set in **Portfolio > Settings**, **Scoring criteria** tab: adapt them once, they apply to every request. After the conversion, the request shows **Converted**.

## 2. Your project

**The project of your request**, opened by the step

**What to do.** Complete its owners and project team in the properties panel, then open the **Timeline** and **Effort** tabs.

**What you see.** The project keeps the request it comes from under **Relations**. **Timeline** holds its phases, which you can start from a phase template. **Effort** sets its progress against the effort planned and consumed.

## 3. The first tasks

**Portfolio > Tasks**

**What to do.** Click **New**, create a task and link it to the project. Assign it to yourself and add a comment. Change its status, and log time with **Log time**.

**What you see.** The task in the list and on the project's **Tasks** tab. The time you log counts in the project's effort.

## 4. The project's budget

**The project's Relations tab**, opened by the step

**What to do.** Under budget lines, link one or more lines to the project. A line that does not exist yet is created in **Budget management > Budget lines** with **New line**.

**What you see.** Each linked line with its amount for the year, so the project's costs show on its record.

## 5. Your contributor profile

**Portfolio > Contributors**, your own profile, opened by the step

**What to do.** Set your **Project availability**.

**What you see.** The days per month you give to projects feed the capacity reports.
