# Your IT landscape in KANAP in ten minutes

[Explore with the example](landscape-example.md) · **Start with my own data**

In ten minutes, your locations, your applications, their technologies and your servers are in KANAP, your first end of support dates show, and a colleague can keep the map up to date. Starting with your own data erases the example: what you see at the end comes from you.

These are the five steps of the **Your first five steps** tile on the home page, for the path **The IT landscape map** with **Start with my own data**. Each step in the tile opens its screen with a short hint. Each step creates something and is marked done as soon as it exists in the workspace. To mark a step you skip as done, click the mark next to it. See [Trial: your path and first steps](../my-dashboard.md#trial-first-steps).

## 1. Your locations

**IT landscape > Locations**

**What to do.** Click **Add location** and create your server rooms, data centers or clouds: a code, a name and a hosting type.

**What you see.** Every server belongs to a location, which gives it its provider, hosting type and country. A location missing later can also be created from the **Location** field of a server.

## 2. Your applications

**IT landscape > Applications**

**What to do.** Either import the list of your applications, or create them one by one.

- **Import a file.** Click **Import**, then **Download an example**: a workbook with a few applications and what each column takes. Fill in yours, then **Import** the file.
- **Create an application.** Click **New application**, type its name in the title and press Enter. Fill in the properties panel, then click **Create**. **Cancel** drops it.

**What you see.** One record per application. Complete the criticality and the **IT owners** of the most important ones first.

## 3. Your technologies

**IT landscape > Technologies**

**What to do.** Click **New technology** and pick a product in the **Catalog**, for example your database. Then open an application, **Operations** tab, and under **Technologies** click **Add** to link the technology at its version.

**What you see.** Your workspace already holds Windows Server and Ubuntu. A technology taken from the catalog comes with its versions and their end of support dates, kept up to date by KANAP. The end of support then shows on the application, and **IT landscape > End of support** lists what reaches it.

## 4. Your servers

**IT landscape > Assets**

**What to do.** Click **Add asset**, or **Import** a file for many servers. Choose the asset type and the location, then **Create**. To link a server to an application, open the application, **Deployments** tab: **Add deployment** for the environment, then **Add server**.

**What you see.** The application's deployment block lists its servers, and the server lists the application under **Hosted applications** in its **Operations** tab. The end of support and the vulnerabilities of its technologies now reach the server.

## 5. A colleague

**Admin > Users**

**What to do.** Click **New**, enter the **Email** of an application or asset owner, choose **IT Landscape Member** under **Roles**, then **Save and invite**. Add them to the **IT owners** of their application.

**What you see.** Your colleague shows with the status **Invited** and receives an email with a link to choose a password. **IT Landscape Member** can change applications and infrastructure.
