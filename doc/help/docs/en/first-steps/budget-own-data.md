# Your IT budget in KANAP in ten minutes

[Explore with the example](budget-example.md) · **Start with my own data**

In ten minutes, your company, your budget lines and a contract are in KANAP, your first report runs on your figures, and a colleague can read them. Starting with your own data erases the example: what you see at the end comes from you.

These are the five steps of the **Your first five steps** tile on the home page, for the path **The IT budget** with **Start with my own data**. Each step in the tile opens its screen with a short hint. A step that creates something is marked done as soon as it exists in the workspace. For **Your company** and **Your first report**, click **Done** in the hint, or the mark next to the step. See [Trial: your path and first steps](../my-dashboard.md#trial-first-steps).

## 1. Your company

**Master data > Companies**

**What to do.** Open the company created with your workspace. Check its **Base currency** and its **Chart of accounts**, then complete the other fields. Add your own chart in **Master data > Charts of accounts** if the one proposed does not fit. Create your other companies with **New**.

**What you see.** The company is the legal entity that pays. It sets the currency of its lines and the chart of accounts their accounts come from.

## 2. Your budget lines

**Budget management > Budget lines**

**What to do.** Either import your budget file, or create a first line by hand.

- **Import a file.** Click **Import**, then **Download an example**: a workbook with a few sample lines and what each column takes. Fill in your lines and import the file. On an empty list, **Export** gives the header row alone.
- **Create a line.** Click **New line**, type its name in the title and press Enter. Fill in the **Properties** panel: paying company, account, currency. Enter the amounts in the **Budget** tab, then click **Create**. **Cancel** drops the line.

**What you see.** An import checks the whole file and reports the lines to create, to update and those unchanged. A file with one error loads nothing. A line created by hand shows in the list with its amounts for the year.

## 3. A linked contract

**Budget management > Contracts**

**What to do.** Click **New contract**, fill in the form, then **Create**. In the contract's **Budget** tab, link the line it pays for with the picker under the table.

**What you see.** The contract dates each term, tells you when a renewal decision is due, and compares what it commits with the budget lines linked to it.

## 4. Your first report

**Budget management > Reporting > Top lines**

**What to do.** Check that the filters follow your entries: **Year**, OPEX or CAPEX, **Top count**. Add more lines to enrich the reports.

**What you see.** Your largest lines of the year with their share of the total. A click on a line opens it.

## 5. A colleague

**Admin > Users**

**What to do.** Click **New**, enter your colleague's **Email**, choose a role under **Roles**, for example **Budget Reader**, then **Save and invite**. To let your colleagues sign in with Microsoft Entra ID, set up SSO in **Admin > Authentication**.

**What you see.** Your colleague shows with the status **Invited** and receives an email with a link to choose a password. **Budget Reader** reads the lines, the contracts and the reports without changing them.
