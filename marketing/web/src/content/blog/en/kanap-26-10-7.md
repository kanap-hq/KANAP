The controller of your Italian subsidiary needs the Italian budget, and only that. The head of a business unit wants to follow the lines of their cost centers without reading the salaries of the neighbouring department. A contractor is helping you renegotiate the network contracts and should see those contracts, nothing more.

Until now, KANAP gave each person access to whole modules: whoever could read the budget read all of it. With 26.10.7, you decide which part of the budget and contracts each person sees, and whether they can change it.

## Limit a person to their share of the budget

![The Data access tab of a user limits Luca Ferrari to the Italian subsidiary, in edit, with a preview of what this access covers.](/screenshots/release-26-10-7-user-data-access.png)

**Choose a scope, in read or edit.** An administrator opens a user and goes to the new **Data access** tab. By default, everyone keeps **All data**. Switch to **Limited to**, then add companies, cost center groups or single cost centers, each in **Read** or **Edit**. Before you save, a preview counts what the person will see: so many budget lines, so many of them editable, so many contracts.

**Or start from the company.** The same access can be given from the other side: the **Access** tab of a company or a cost center lists who sees it, directly or through a parent group, and adds a person in a few clicks.

**Their lines, everywhere.** A limited person sees only their budget lines and contracts, wherever they look: budget lists and totals, contracts, reports, search, exports, Plaid, the AI assistant built into KANAP, and your own AI tools connected over MCP. A report run by the Italian controller adds up Italian figures, and Plaid answers with Italian lines.

**The rest of the estate stays shared.** Applications, servers and projects are read as before. A person without budget rights sees that an application has three linked budget lines, without seeing which ones or their amounts.

![Administration > Data access lists the limited people, their roles, their scopes and the last change.](/screenshots/release-26-10-7-admin-data-access.png)

**One page to keep track.** **Administration > Data access** lists every limited person with their scopes and the last change, filters on "Who has access to…" a company or cost center, and exports or imports the whole set as a CSV file. Setting up twenty controllers takes one file.

Three typical cases:

- **The subsidiary controller** edits the budget of their company and reads nothing else.
- **The business unit head** reads the lines of their cost center group and edits those of their own center.
- **The contractor** reads the contracts and lines of the scope they work on.

Roles still say what a person can do; data access says on which part. The full rules, with examples, are in the [Data access page of the manual](https://doc.kanap.net/data-access/).

If you host KANAP yourself, turning on data access takes a one-time step on the database, described in the [installation guide](https://doc.kanap.net/on-premise/installation/#optional-data-scoped-access). Without it, everyone keeps all data.

## Clusters, described once

![A SQL Server cluster record lists its two member servers and the applications it hosts.](/screenshots/release-26-10-7-cluster.png)

A vSphere farm or a SQL Server cluster used to be described server by server: every application and every network flow repeated on each node. Now the cluster carries them. Assign an application to the cluster and all its members host it, including a node you add next year. A connection can start from a cluster, end at it or pass through it, and applies to each member in service.

The views that count servers count the members: vulnerabilities, end of support, maps and the impact view. On a member's record, "If this server goes down" shows the cluster as degraded, with the applications that keep running on the other nodes.

## And every day

- **A clearer asset record**, with its properties in three groups and tabs for operations, network, hardware and dependencies.
- **CSV imports that check everything before writing.** A wrong host name, an unknown owner or a duplicate is reported before any change. The **Replace** mode changes only the columns present in the file.
- **Confirmations where they matter.** Irreversible actions ask for confirmation, and leaving a modified record offers **Save and leave**, **Leave without saving** or **Stay**.
- **Plaid gives dates and times in your time zone.**
- **Your language throughout**: exports, error messages, labels and number formats follow the interface language.

This release also brings security updates to the API.

## Upgrading

On the hosted service, there is nothing to do: you are already on 26.10.7.

If you host KANAP and follow the stable branch, go straight to 26.10.7, after reading the on-premise section of the [release notes](https://github.com/kanap-hq/KANAP/releases/tag/v26.10.7). If your installation predates 26.10.1, move it to `stable` first, as the [operations guide](https://doc.kanap.net/on-premise/operations/#upgrade-procedure) explains.
