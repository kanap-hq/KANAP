# Kadratis trademark policy

Kadratis is open source software published by KANAP EURL under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3. The license covers the code. It does not cover the name Kadratis or the Kadratis logo: section 7(e) of the AGPL lets a licensor decline to grant trademark rights, and we do.

This policy says how you may use the name and the logo. We want everyone to be able to talk about Kadratis, build on it, distribute it and earn a living from it. We also want an IT department to know, when it installs or buys "Kadratis", that it gets the software we publish and the security fixes we ship.

If you use the name in good faith and nobody could mistake you for us, you are almost certainly fine. When in doubt, ask: we would rather say yes.

## The marks

This policy covers:

- the name **Kadratis**;
- the name **Kadratis Cloud**, our hosted offer;
- the **Kadratis logo**.

KANAP is the name of the company that publishes Kadratis.

## What you may do without asking

- **Use the software.** Install, run, study, modify and redistribute Kadratis under the terms of the AGPL. This policy takes nothing away from the license.
- **Talk about Kadratis.** Write articles, reviews, comparisons, tutorials, books, courses and talks about it, and name it in them.
- **Say what your work does with it.** "Integration of Kadratis with GLPI", "compatible with Kadratis", "migrating from spreadsheets to Kadratis", "Kadratis consultant" on a profile or a CV.
- **Redistribute Kadratis unchanged, under its name.** The source code or the official container images, mirrored, packaged for a platform or deployed for a client, as long as what the software does is unchanged.
- **Run Kadratis for yourself or for your clients.** An internal instance, an installation on a client's servers, or a hosted instance you operate keeps the name in the interface, as shipped. An address such as `kadratis.example.com` for your own instance is fine.
- **Sell services around Kadratis**: installation, configuration, data migration, training, hosting, support. Put your own name first and Kadratis after it, as a description: "Acme, hosting for Kadratis", "Kadratis training by Acme". Say that you are independent of KANAP.
- **Use the unaltered logo** to illustrate an article, a talk or a review about Kadratis.

## What needs our written agreement

- **A modified version under the name Kadratis.** If you distribute a modified version, or offer one as a service, give it another name, not confusingly similar (no "Kadratis Plus", no "Kadratix"), and remove the logo. You may say that it is "based on Kadratis" or "a fork of Kadratis". The name shown in the interface is set in two files, `backend/src/config/product.ts` and `frontend/src/config/product.ts`. The copyright notices and the license stay, as the AGPL requires.
- **Kadratis in the name of your product, company, service or hosted offer**, for example "Kadratis Hosting", "MyKadratis" or "Kadratis Pro Services".
- **Kadratis in a domain name, a social media account, or an app or package name** that could be taken for ours, such as `kadratis-cloud.com` or `@kadratis_support`. A subdomain of your own domain for your own instance is fine (see above).
- **Anything that suggests endorsement or official status**: "official", "certified", "partner", "recommended by KANAP", unless we have agreed to it in writing.
- **Any other use of the logo**, and any modified version of it.
- **Registering the marks**, or a confusingly similar sign, as a trademark or a company name, anywhere.
