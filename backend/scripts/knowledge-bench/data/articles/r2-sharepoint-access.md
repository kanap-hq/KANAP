Team documents live on **SharePoint** sites (Finance, Quality, Production, Sales). The old network drives (S:, Q:) are read-only since the move to SharePoint.

## Requesting access

When you open a link to a site you cannot see, SharePoint shows **"You need permission to access this item"** with a **Request access** button. Click it and add a short reason: the request goes to the **site owner**, not to IT. Owners usually answer within a day.

If nobody answers after two business days, open a ticket under **IT > Access > SharePoint** with the site address; the service desk finds the owner or a backup owner.

## Sharing with someone outside the company

External sharing is allowed only on sites marked *Partners* (for example *Quality-Suppliers*). On the other sites, the **Share** button only offers internal people.

## Syncing a library on your laptop

Open the library, click **Sync**, and the files appear in the File Explorer under *Fromage & Co*. Do not sync the whole Production site: it holds more than 200 000 files and slows the laptop down.

## Former network drives

The S: and Q: drives remain readable until the end of the year for reference. New files must go to SharePoint.
