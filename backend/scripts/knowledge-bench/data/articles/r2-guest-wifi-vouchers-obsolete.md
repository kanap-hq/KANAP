**Obsolete: replaced by the self-service guest portal.**

Visitors ask reception for a **paper voucher** with a Wi-Fi code. Reception prints vouchers from the *Guest Manager* console on the reception PC.

1. Open Guest Manager and choose the number of vouchers.
2. Select the duration: 4 hours, 1 day or 1 week.
3. Print and hand the vouchers to the visitors; each voucher works on one device only.

Vouchers for more than 10 people must be requested from IT by email one week ahead. Lost vouchers cannot be reprinted: issue a new one.
