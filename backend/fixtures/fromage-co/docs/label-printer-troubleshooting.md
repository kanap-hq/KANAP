# Label Printer Troubleshooting (Zebra)

The warehouses and production sites use **Zebra ZT411** printers for product and shipping labels. Paris, Gouda and Parma each keep two spare label rolls per format in the IT cabinet.

## Jams and curled labels

Curled or jamming labels are almost always an **environment problem**, not a printer problem:

1. **Humidity** — label stock absorbs moisture and curls. Do not store rolls (or the printer) next to washing lines, brine baths or cave doors. Relocate the printer or the stock to a dry shelf and the jams stop within a day or two of using a fresh roll.
2. **Cold stock** — rolls stored below 10 °C curl when they warm up in the printer. Let a roll acclimatize for a few hours before loading.
3. **Wrong roll orientation** — labels must unwind from the top, adhesive side down.

If a roll is already curled, discard it — re-feeding curled labels also misaligns the print head calibration.

## Calibration after a jam

1. Clear the jam and reload the roll.
2. Hold **Pause + Cancel** for 2 seconds to run auto-calibration (the printer feeds ~3 blank labels).
3. Print a test label from the menu (**Print > Print Station > Test Label**).

## When to call for help

If the printer still jams with a fresh, dry, acclimatized roll, open a ticket for **Production IT** with the printer serial number (sticker on the side). Hardware service is covered by the Axians managed-services contract — do not order a replacement printer directly; a technician usually revives them, and the procurement team appreciates not buying a new printer every time the Gouda washing line gets enthusiastic.
