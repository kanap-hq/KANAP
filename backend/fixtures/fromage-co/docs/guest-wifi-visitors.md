# Guest Wi-Fi & Visitor Access

## Guest Wi-Fi

Visitors use the **FromageGuest** SSID, available in all offices and the Paris tasting room.

1. The host (any employee) opens the **Guest portal** at `guest.fromage-co.example` and registers the visitors: name, company, visit date.
2. Each visitor receives an access code by email or SMS, valid for the visit day (up to 5 days for auditors).
3. Codes work immediately — no IT ticket needed for groups under 20 people.

For larger events (harvest fair, customer days), open a ServiceNow request at least **3 business days** ahead so IT can extend the guest network capacity.

FromageGuest is internet-only: no access to SAP, file shares or the cave network. Bandwidth is fine for video calls and slide decks.

## Visitor badges and site access

- Office and tasting-room visitors: reception issues a **day badge**; the host escorts at all times.
- **Aging caves**: visits are allowed and honestly the highlight of any customer meeting — but only with an escort from Production, hairnets and cave overshoes (provided at the cave entrance). Book the slot with the Production office one day ahead; groups are capped at 8 so the Comté can maintain its composure.
- Photography is allowed in the caves **without flash** — the cheeses are sensitive to paparazzi.

## Frequent question

*"My visitors arrive Thursday — is it too late?"* No: guest Wi-Fi codes take effect immediately and a cave slot booked Wednesday for Thursday is usually fine outside the September–October affinage peak.
